## Research Projects & Systems

- **[Biomedical Resource Ontology (BRO) & Resource Discovery System](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2010.10.003).** Federated, inter-institutional ontology and discovery system enabling semantic annotation and search of biomedical resources across CTSA institutions.
- **[University of Michigan Honest Broker](https://doi.org/10.1197/jamia.M2985).** Web Services-based platform for sharing de-identified clinical and research data across medical disciplines and institutions while preserving patient privacy.
- **[VISAGE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3041531/).** Visual Aggregator and Explorer: a query interface for clinical research with ontology-driven query building, management, and exploratory data mining for cohort identification.
- **[Ribosomal Database Project (RDP-II)](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/29/1/173/1116006).** Aligned and annotated rRNA sequence database with phylogenetic tree derivation and analysis services, supporting microbial taxonomy worldwide.
- **[Biodegradative Strain Database (BSD)](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkg032).** Web-accessible database of degradative bacteria and the hazardous substances they degrade, with linked literature, patents, and chemical data.
- **[Ribosomal RNA Operon Copy Number Database (rrnDB)](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/29/1/181/1116185).** Phylogenetically arranged database of rRNA operon copy number across prokaryotes, supporting evolutionary and microbial community analysis.
- **[Terminal Restriction Fragment Length Polymorphism Analysis Program (TAP T-RFLP)](https://doi.org/10.1128/aem.66.8.3616-3620.2000).** Web-based research tool for in silico restriction digestion of 16S rDNA sequences, enabling microbial community analysis and genetic fingerprinting.

## Open-Source Projects

- **[aimu](https://github.com/saxman/aimu).** A Python library (AI Modeling Utilities) for finding out what generative AI models and systems are actually capable of, and understanding how they work. One provider-agnostic interface spans text, image, audio, and speech across local and cloud backends (Ollama, Hugging Face, llama.cpp, Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini). Includes agents and code-controlled workflows, typed streaming that labels reasoning, tool calls, and output separately, MCP tools and agent skills, semantic and document memory, prompt tuning, benchmarking, and a full async mirror.
- **[kokua](https://github.com/saxman/kokua).** A hackable, always-on personal AI assistant built on aimu, designed to show how agentic systems actually work. It chats in the terminal or the browser, writes and runs its own skills, connects to remote MCP services, delegates subtasks to isolated sub-agents, schedules its own proactive work, and remembers facts and documents across conversations, streaming reasoning, tool calls, and results as they happen.
- **[genscai](https://github.com/saxman/genscai).** Recipes and notebooks applying generative AI to science and research workflows, focused on health and infectious disease modeling: automated literature retrieval, information extraction, document embedding and clustering with science-specific models (SPECTER, NV-Embed), classification, knowledge graph construction, and agent development.
